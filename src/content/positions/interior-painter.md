We are hiring a skilled Commercial Painter to prepare surfaces and apply paint to large buildings, offices, and retail spaces. You will work safely with various coatings, use professional tools, and deliver clean, high-quality finishes on time. Pay ranges from $22 to $32 per hour based on experience.

**Job Responsibilities**

- Surface Prep: Clean, sand, patch, and prime walls, ceilings, and exterior surfaces.
- Painting: Apply paint, stain, and coatings using brushes, rollers, and sprayers.
- Safety: Set up ladders, scaffolding, and safety gear following strict workplace rules.
- Clean-Up: Protect surrounding areas with drop cloths, tape, and plastic, then clean up daily.
